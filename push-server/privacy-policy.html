Privacy Policy — Mini Shorts

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Shorts ("the app", "we", "us"), operated by Panda Money, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using Mini Shorts, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the app:

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, Facebook, or your phone number, our authentication provider (Google Firebase) receives your name, email address, and/or phone number to create and secure your account.
  • Device / notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a push-notification token so we can alert you when new stories are published.
  • App preferences. Your reading choices — bookmarks, likes, reading history, topics you follow or mute, theme, and language — are stored on your device and are not used to identify you.
  • Basic usage data. Standard, non-identifying diagnostic and performance data may be collected to keep the app stable.

2. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To personalize your feed and remember your preferences.
  • To send notifications about new content (only if you opt in).
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, including:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and, where used, related services.
  • Expo — app delivery and push-notification transport.
  • Google / Facebook — only when you choose to sign in with them, and only as needed to complete sign-in.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Data retention

We keep account information for as long as your account is active. Preferences stored on your device remain until you clear them or uninstall the app.

5. Your choices & rights

  • You can sign out at any time from the Profile screen.
  • You can turn notifications on or off from your device settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us (see below).

6. Children’s privacy

Mini Shorts is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including our authentication provider’s security, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact us

For any privacy question or a data-deletion request, contact us at accounts@getpanda.money.
